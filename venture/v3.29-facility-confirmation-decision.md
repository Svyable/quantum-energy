# v3.29 Facility Confirmation Decision Gate

## Decision

Do not treat public facility relevance, equipment ownership, accreditation, or a favorable webpage audit as execution readiness. Require a completed v3.29 direct-confirmation response plus a dry-run v3.27 evidence packet before committing the full R2 campaign.

## Business value

This reduces the risk of spending schedule/capital on a facility that cannot preserve configuration continuity, raw-data provenance, prospective holdout discipline, or the exact R2 temporal/spectral protocol.

## Gate

A candidate may advance to dry-run packet exchange only when:

- all 8 global questions are answered;
- all 14 capability questions are answered;
- no required answer is `NO` for capabilities assigned to that facility;
- all `CONDITIONAL` answers have explicit, testable conditions;
- evidence/contact provenance is recorded for affirmative claims;
- data-rights, shipping/EHS, sample-acceptance, and access constraints are explicit.

A candidate remains `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` while any required answer is `UNKNOWN`.

## Multi-facility rule

If no single facility can satisfy the complete contract, do not average partial capability scores. Design an explicit bridge study with configuration/provenance transfer and additional uncertainty terms before splitting execution.

## Claim boundary

Passing this gate establishes only partner-workflow readiness. It does not establish R2 performance, EPC, open-quantum transport, commercial PV performance, or a contractual partnership.

## Next increment

Apply the frozen questionnaire unchanged to the candidate set and commit the dated responses before choosing a facility route.
