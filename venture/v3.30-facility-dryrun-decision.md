# v3.30 Facility Dry-Run Decision Gate

## Decision

Do not schedule the full R2 scientific campaign solely from a favorable facility questionnaire response. Require a small candidate-specific dry-run/export packet to pass the v3.30 structural validator first.

## Why this is commercially useful

The current bottleneck is integration risk rather than another synthetic physics model. A facility may possess suitable instruments yet return data in a form that breaks frozen provenance, configuration, raw-data, or holdout requirements. Detecting that incompatibility before paid scientific acquisition reduces rework and protects interpretability.

## Gate

A candidate can advance from written capability confirmation to scientific scheduling only when:

1. every claimed v3.27 evidence role has an example/dry-run object;
2. the 15-role manifest is complete or unavailable roles are explicitly `MISSING`;
3. SHA-256 and byte counts match actual files;
4. paths are safe and roles unique;
5. provenance/configuration fields can be returned without undocumented manual reconstruction;
6. the packet status is `STRUCTURAL_PASS`.

`STRUCTURAL_PASS` is an integration gate only. It must never be presented as evidence that the facility's measurements pass metrology criteria.

## Kill / narrow rules

- Any missing material role -> keep facility route `INCOMPLETE` until resolved or design an explicit multi-facility bridge.
- Any integrity contradiction -> `FAIL`; do not begin scientific analysis.
- Required transformations that cannot be documented/versioned -> narrow the interoperability claim and build an adapter first.
- A vendor/lab reputation, accreditation, or “yes” response does not substitute for the dry-run packet.

## Cost / schedule boundary

No vendor price, schedule, or savings estimate is claimed. The value proposition is qualitative risk retirement: discover export/provenance incompatibility before scarce samples or paid acquisition are consumed.

## Best next increment

Apply the frozen v3.29 questionnaire and v3.30 packet rehearsal as a paired preflight to the first responding facility. Only then compare candidate scheduling/cost terms under an unchanged scientific contract.
