# v3.32 facility time / quote decision

## Decision

Use a **complete-protocol, parameterized instrument-time model** when requesting or comparing facility quotes. Do not compare facilities on a partial service-line price that omits required v3.27 evidence roles or prospective validation work.

## Why this matters commercially

A low headline measurement price can be false economy if calibration repeatability, spectral characterization, settling qualification, raw export, or provenance work is omitted and must later be repeated elsewhere. v3.32 makes the protocol burden explicit before a commercial commitment.

## Synthetic planning result

With deliberately synthetic low/nominal/high timing inputs, modeled complete-protocol burden is:

- low: 2.9306 h;
- nominal: 7.1906 h;
- high: 19.9733 h.

Reference-repeatability work contributes 45.5%, 53.8%, and 63.4% respectively.

These values are **not vendor quotes, facility throughput measurements, or project-duration commitments**.

## Quote-normalization rule

For facility `f`, define:

`C_complete,f = C_fixed,f + r_f * T_total,f + C_external,f`,

where:

- `C_fixed,f` = explicitly quoted fixed setup/admin charge [currency];
- `r_f` = explicitly quoted hourly instrument/staff rate [currency/hour];
- `T_total,f` = v3.32 complete-protocol hours using facility-provided timing inputs [hour];
- `C_external,f` = explicitly quoted required external services/shipping [currency].

Dimensional check: `(currency/hour)*hour = currency`; all additive terms are currency.

No numerical cost is computed until real quoted values exist. Unknown charges remain `UNKNOWN`, not zero.

## Gate

A facility can enter cost comparison only when:

1. required v3.27 capabilities/roles are confirmed or explicitly conditional;
2. timing-template fields are supplied with dated provenance;
3. exclusions from the requested protocol are explicit;
4. required external/subcontracted measurements are separated from in-house capability;
5. raw-data/provenance deliverables are included;
6. quote validity date and configuration applicability are recorded.

If any material cost/timing term is missing, commercial status is `INCOMPLETE` rather than cheapest-by-assumption.

## Sensitivity / decision robustness

The synthetic time scenarios span a factor of ~6.8 in total modeled hours (19.9733 / 2.9306). This is an engineering sensitivity ratio, not an uncertainty confidence bound. It shows that throughput assumptions can materially reorder facility cost even before hourly rates differ.

Because the calibration campaign is multiplied across 30 sessions and 2520 records, modest per-point/session overhead differences can dominate schedule and cost. Facility selection should therefore optimize complete evidence delivery and qualified throughput rather than a single instrument's nominal speed.

## Safety/environment

No schedule or quote optimization can override facility interlocks, source duty limits, instrument ratings, thermal stabilization, handling, or EHS requirements. Required safety-controlled delay is a valid timing input, not waste to remove.
