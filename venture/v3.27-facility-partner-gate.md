# v3.27 Facility Partner Gate

## Venture purpose

The program's immediate bottleneck is no longer the absence of another synthetic R2 metrology model. It is the ability to identify and execute with a facility that can return the complete evidence packet without breaking prospective validation, configuration control, or provenance.

## Gate

A candidate partner is `READY_FOR_SCHEDULING` only when every decision-critical capability in `technical/data/r2_facility_capability_contract_v3_27.json` is either:

- directly confirmed by the facility for the intended configuration; or
- supplied by an explicitly named second facility through a documented provenance/configuration bridge that does not invalidate the relevant gate.

Otherwise status is `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION` or `NOT_READY`.

This status is operational only. It is not a scientific or commercial-performance claim.

## Partner selection principles

Prefer facilities that can:

- preserve one coherent source/detector/instrument/software configuration across prerequisite gates;
- export raw/minimally processed machine-readable data;
- provide calibration and spectral-responsivity provenance;
- support prospective freeze/holdout discipline;
- retain failed and excluded measurements with reason codes;
- permit public release of non-confidential metrology methods/results where legally possible;
- operate within ordinary facility safety and interlock procedures without custom unsafe exceptions.

Do not select a partner primarily because of brand recognition, publication count, or generic claims of advanced spectroscopy.

## Commercial consequence

If no single partner can satisfy the complete contract, the program should split the measurement workflow only where the scientific state can be transferred without invalidating calibration or temporal/spectral qualification. The burden of proving that bridge belongs to the program; it must not be assumed.

## Kill / narrow rule

If a facility cannot provide material calibration/provenance or cannot freeze the acquisition configuration required for a gate, narrow the requested experiment rather than accepting unverifiable results.

If the required metrology is impractical at available facilities, that is a program constraint and should trigger redesign of the measurement claim or instrument architecture before proprietary materials spend.

## Next decision

Build a dated, source-linked candidate-facility matrix against v3.27. Keep public capability evidence separate from direct-confirmation evidence and unresolved questions. Only then initiate partner outreach/scheduling for the full R2 campaign.
