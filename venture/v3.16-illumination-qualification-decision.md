# v3.16 Deployment Decision — Qualify Illumination Before Mechanism Spend

## Decision

Do not treat the v3.15 high synthetic power as a reason to release a larger R2 mechanism campaign until the facility passes the v3.16 illumination-axis and sweep-history qualification.

## Why this is capital-efficient

The v3.16 synthetic stress shows that a common intensity-scale error can cancel from the derivative observable while a small smooth nonlinearity can create a materially larger curvature bias. Therefore buying more devices/substrates before bounding the calibration axis can spend fabrication money on a measurement artifact.

The cheapest next evidence is a reference-detector calibration sweep, temperature logging, and ascending/descending repeat on stable reference/R2 devices. These measurements retire or bound a major failure mode before proprietary D18/PY-IT/eC9 fabrication.

## Gate to further spend

Proceed from R2 metrology qualification to a confirmatory mechanism campaign only if:

- v3.16 Q1–Q5 pass prospectively;
- the measured covariance/noise is fed back into the frozen v3.15 power model;
- the resulting independent-substrate sample size still clears the preregistered power/recovery gate;
- no sweep-history or thermal confound changes the sign/interpretation of the primary curvature observable.

If the facility cannot meet these conditions, invest first in source/reference calibration and acquisition control rather than increasing substrate count.

## Claim boundary

A passed calibration certificate is operational metrology evidence only. It is not evidence of EPC, open-quantum transport, a proprietary mechanism, improved Pmax, or commercial advantage.
