# v3.21 deployment decision — external systematics before facility mechanism claims

## Decision

Do not treat repeatability-qualified R2 calibration as a complete uncertainty budget until external/reference systematic terms are represented separately and propagated through the actual curvature observable.

## Why this changes execution

Repeated sweeps can estimate repeatability covariance but cannot reveal a bias shared by all repetitions of the same reference system. v3.21 therefore adds a distinct external-systematic packet consisting of recognized corrections, uncertainty convention/coverage factor, signed intensity-grid shape, provenance, date/version, and validity state.

The business consequence is favorable: this prevents unnecessary spending on the wrong calibration quantity. In the synthetic stress test, common absolute scale contributes essentially nothing to the derivative curvature uncertainty while intensity-dependent shape dominates. A facility qualification package should therefore report the component-level sensitivity before buying tighter instrumentation or calibration service.

## Procurement / partner requirement

A cooperating facility should be able to supply, where applicable:

- reference-detector calibration certificate/report identifier and date/version;
- uncertainty convention and coverage factor;
- detector/source range and configuration scope;
- applied correction factors or enough information to calculate them;
- detector linearity/nonlinearity characterization over the relevant intensity range;
- source-spectrum and spectral-mismatch characterization;
- detector temperature/geometry dependencies if material;
- explicit statement of terms that remain manufacturer/literature/engineering assumptions.

Absence of one of these is not automatically a failure if the term is demonstrably immaterial, but it must be recorded rather than silently assigned zero uncertainty.

## Open-source value

The new CSV interface lets different laboratories replace project assumptions with their own traceable systematic components without modifying the curvature-analysis code. It also exposes which uncertainty source dominates the decision-driving measurand, making calibration effort transferable rather than facility-specific folklore.

## Capital gate

No new metrology capex is justified by v3.21 alone. First run the component sensitivity using existing facility certificates and characterization. Buy improved calibration/linearity/spectral characterization only if the propagated component materially limits the preregistered R2 decision.

## Claim boundary

Passing v3.21 supports only completeness/traceability of the declared external uncertainty budget. It does not establish absolute detector truth beyond the supplied evidence, and it does not support an EPC, recombination, open-quantum, or commercial-performance claim.
