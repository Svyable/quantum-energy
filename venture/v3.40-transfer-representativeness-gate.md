# v3.40 venture gate — transfer logger representativeness

## Decision

Do not spend qualified R2 primaries or cross-facility measurement time on a transfer campaign merely because the logger is time-complete or bandwidth-qualified. The chosen logger placement must also be shown, on a dummy package, to represent the device location within a prospectively declared engineering requirement.

## Allowed evidence states

- `QUALIFIED_FOR_DECLARED_REPRESENTATIVENESS`: proceed only for the tested package/carrier/logger geometry and declared thresholds.
- `FAIL_REPRESENTATIVENESS`: redesign sensor placement/package geometry or use a device-adjacent sensor before primary transfer.
- `INCOMPLETE`: do not treat exposure records as evidence of device-local conditions.

## Cost/claim boundary

This protocol has no vendor quote and introduces no cost estimate. A low-cost logger that cannot bound device-local exposure is not a cheaper equivalent to a qualified transfer configuration because it leaves a conventional confound unresolved.

Passing v3.40 does not establish safe shipping, electrical stability, metrology transfer, facility equivalence, or a quantum mechanism. It only qualifies a logger placement as a bounded proxy under the tested dummy-package conditions and prospectively declared thresholds.

## Commercial value

The value is avoiding false facility disagreements and preventable repeat campaigns. A cross-site result is more transferable when transport-induced environmental state change is bounded separately from facility measurement differences.

## Next spend gate

Before shipping qualified R2 primaries, require a combined dummy-package record showing both v3.39 temporal fidelity and v3.40 placement representativeness, plus the existing carrier/handling qualification and transfer-environment provenance. Unknown thresholds or missing raw records remain `INCOMPLETE`, never zero-cost assumptions.
