# v3.3 deployment/capital note — separate reference qualification from mechanism classification

## Business/program delta

The R2 program now has two distinct sample-count purposes:

1. **Reference qualification / fabrication variance:** keep the existing 5-substrate × 2-pixel pilot because its job is to estimate transfer-standard reproducibility and metrology drift.
2. **Confirmatory H1–H4 mechanism classification:** do not assume those same five substrates are sufficient. The v3.3 synthetic recovery study makes this layer exploratory at N=5 under nominal assumptions.

This separation prevents a common capital mistake: either overbuilding the reference pilot merely to support a later physics claim, or underpowering the physics claim because the reference pilot already exists.

## Planning scaling

If variable fabrication/FTPS/EL effort scales approximately linearly with independent substrate count, moving a mechanism audit from 5 to 9 substrates increases the substrate-dependent portion of the campaign by:

`(9 / 5) - 1 = 0.80`, or **80%**.

This is a planning scaling relationship only. Fixed setup/calibration costs do not scale this way, and no dollar quote is inferred.

The preferred decision is therefore **not automatically 'fabricate nine'**. First test whether a second independent H1 discriminator (temperature-dependent FTPS/EL broadening) can raise recovery enough to reduce required N. An added observable may have higher information-per-dollar than four additional full device/substrate measurement packages.

## Open-source deployment implication

For public release, sample-count sufficiency should be tied to the exact claim:

- R2 reproducibility result: publish the 5-substrate hierarchical pilot with its confidence bounds.
- Mechanism claim: publish only after the exact design passes the committed synthetic recovery gate.
- If recovery remains ambiguous, publish the ambiguity and raw data rather than collapsing mechanisms into a preferred narrative.

## IP / openness

No proprietary claim is attached to the sample-size rule or classifier. The simulation code, confusion matrices, assumptions, and negative result are public so other laboratories can challenge the assumptions and rerun the decision with their own metrology noise.

## Current stop rule

Do not expand physical mechanism-audit sample count until the next synthetic increment compares:

- more independent substrates; versus
- an orthogonal temperature-dependent disorder discriminator; versus
- a lower demonstrated metrology-noise regime.

Select the design with the best preregistered per-class recovery per unit of experimental burden.
