# Venture decision — v3.33 gate abort and evidence salvage

## Decision

Use dependency-aware abort semantics for any future R2 facility execution. A failed or incomplete prerequisite blocks its descendants but should not automatically terminate independent metrology branches that remain scientifically useful and safely executable.

## Business value

This prevents two costly failure modes:

1. **false continuation** — paying for downstream acquisition whose prerequisite metrology already failed, then discovering the result cannot support the intended claim;
2. **false total-abort** — throwing away independent calibration/spectral evidence that could diagnose the failure, improve the next run, or transfer to another facility/configuration.

No expected-dollar savings are claimed because real failure probabilities, facility rates, and configuration-specific times are not yet measured. Open PR #24 separately models synthetic timing scenarios; v3.33 remains probability- and price-free.

## Commercial integrity gate

A facility result may enter mechanism/product diligence only if every dependency required for that result has explicit `PASS` status under the frozen configuration. `INCOMPLETE` is not a discountable uncertainty term and `FAIL` is not removable by omission.

## Negative-result value

Failed prospective holdout, temporal-fidelity, settling, spectral, or randomized-order gates remain valuable diligence artifacts. They narrow the measurement system, expose redesign needs, and prevent unsupported capital escalation.

## Safety

Execution permission from the dependency graph never supersedes facility EHS, interlock, source-duty, temperature, or instrument-rating constraints.

## Next gate

Before committing to a full R2 facility campaign, apply v3.33 to one real or facility-supplied dry-run packet and require human/machine agreement on all stop/continue decisions.
