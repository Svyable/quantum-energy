# v3.78 QG candidate pivot — topology certainty before transport spend

## Decision

Prioritize **polyheptacene (N=7)** for the next same-structure PBE/GW topology adjudication. Retain **polypentacene (N=5)** as the method-sensitivity control.

This is a computational research-priority decision, not a product/material-selection claim.

## Why the priority changed

The v3.77 blocker is discrete: published PBE and cited GW treatments disagree on N=5's topology. The 2025 topology paper places the GW chain-length transition between N=5 and N=7, while its PBE transition lies between N=3 and N=5. Therefore N=7 has cross-study topological label agreement and N=5 does not.

Cross-study agreement is weaker than same-structure method consensus, so the next spend is an adjudication calculation rather than fabrication.

## The transport tradeoff

The 2025 transport paper reports external 300 K diffusion values:

- N5 topological: 1.76 cm^2/s;
- N7 topological: 0.44 cm^2/s.

So with equal lifetime N7 has half the N5 diffusion length because `L_D proportional sqrt(D tau)`. N7 would require four times the N5 lifetime to match absolute diffusion reach.

However, N7 has the larger reported relative topological/trivial diffusion enhancement, approximately 4.27x versus 2.89x for N5. This makes N7 attractive as a **mechanism-discrimination candidate**, not automatically as the highest-performing transport material.

## Capital gate

Do not spend into synthesis/fabrication, bespoke strain fixtures or downstream phonon-transport computation merely because N7 has the larger enhancement ratio.

Advance only in this order:

1. retrieve and hash the exact structural coordinates;
2. run converged PBE and GW-level calculations on that identical hash;
3. confirm parity/Berry/Wannier topology by independent paths;
4. determine absolute exciton lifetime and `D*tau`;
5. predict finite-distance sink capture;
6. only then decide whether physical material work outranks the existing OPV program.

## Kill / pivot conditions

Pivot away from N7/polyacenes if:

- converged same-structure GW classifies N7 as trivial;
- independent topological markers disagree;
- classification depends on discretionary convergence settings near a boundary;
- N7 lifetime is far below the ~4x N5 compensation needed to overcome its lower published D and no other useful-work advantage offsets this;
- a less exotic material gives comparable absolute sink delivery, durability and manufacturability with lower scientific/capital burden.

## Platform upside if the gate passes

The attractive long-term architecture remains:

`method-robust topology -> quantum-geometric transport headroom -> environment/dielectric control -> strain or field routing -> irreversible sink`.

That architecture is a hypothesis. v3.78 only improves which material should be used to test it efficiently.
