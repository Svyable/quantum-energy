# Venture gate — R2 transfer environment v3.38

## Decision

Do not treat a cross-facility disagreement, shipping-control result, or apparently successful transfer as investable metrology evidence unless the associated transfer event has a complete environmental/provenance record under v3.38 or a reviewed successor.

## Why this matters commercially

A facility-comparison campaign can consume scarce devices, staff time, shipping cycles and external-lab budget. If transport exposure is undocumented, a failed transfer cannot cleanly diagnose whether the facility, device, package or transport path caused the change. That ambiguity reduces the value of the entire campaign and can force an expensive repeat.

## Spend rule

The exposure ledger is a prerequisite for interpreting transport-sensitive R2 results, but it is not a justification for additional capex. Unknown temperature/RH/shock limits remain `UNKNOWN`, not zero-cost or implicitly acceptable. Any vendor/facility quote should identify who supplies the logger, calibration record, export format, carrier/package constraints and chain-of-custody timestamps.

## Narrowing rule

If a transfer event is `INCOMPLETE`, preserve the measurements but narrow the inference to the combined facility-plus-transfer path. If a documented relevant exposure limit is exceeded, diagnose transport/package/device state before spending on additional mechanism-facing measurements. A complete log within declared limits still does not eliminate unmeasured handling/contact/remounting/ageing confounds.

## Next capital-efficient increment

Qualify one carrier/logger package on dummies using controlled environmental and mechanical transients. Use those data to set a justified logging bandwidth and device-adjacent gradient uncertainty before sending qualified primaries to a second facility.
