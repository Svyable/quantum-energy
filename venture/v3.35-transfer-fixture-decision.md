# v3.35 transfer-fixture decision

## Decision

Treat the R2 carrier/packing interface as prerequisite reproducibility infrastructure for cross-facility work, not as incidental lab hardware.

## Why it matters commercially

An apparent cross-facility shift caused by remounting, handling, particles, contact access, or package damage can waste facility time and corrupt a transferability claim. A standardized low-complexity carrier makes these ordinary explanations more inspectable and transferable to partner labs.

## Spend gate

Do not commission precision hardware or qualified-primary shipment from this specification alone. First human-review dimensions/material compatibility, then make a low-cost prototype/dummy version and pass the frozen dummy-cycle screen. Obtain facility-holder/interface constraints before committing to a production revision.

## Claim boundary

A passing fixture qualification is not a product performance result, facility equivalence result, shipping certification, or quantum-mechanism result.

## Next value step

Prototype v3.35, measure as-built dimensions/preload, document material and contamination compatibility, and execute dummy remount screening. If it fails, redesign before spending on cross-facility R2 acquisition.
