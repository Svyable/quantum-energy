# v3.13 deployment / publication gate

The first external real-data benchmark for the R2 recombination discriminator changes the publication and deployment rule:

- `Voc`-intensity ideality extraction is now benchmarked against a public 2026 NFA-OPV dataset rather than only synthetic curves.
- A single global slope is **not** a stable mechanism label: the public 45% PM6 series shifts from approximately `n=1.10` at 0.05–0.5 sun to `n=0.91` at 0.5–2 sun.
- Therefore R2 must preregister the intensity window and report local curvature / injection dependence rather than selecting a favorable global fit after unblinding.

## Capital consequence

No new capital is released by this benchmark. It reduces interpretation risk in an already planned low-cost measurement. The useful economic consequence is avoiding a false-positive H3 mechanism claim that could otherwise trigger unnecessary proprietary-material or cavity spend.

## Open-source consequence

The executable points to the exact Zenodo source and verifies its published MD5 rather than copying an upstream dataset whose record page does not state an explicit reuse license. This keeps the benchmark reproducible while respecting uncertain redistribution terms.

## Claim level

- arithmetic/regression path: eligible for `reproduced` after CI passes on the checksum-matched external source;
- independent endpoint comparison: eligible for `cross-checked` within its frozen tolerance;
- R2 interface-recombination mechanism: **not established**.
