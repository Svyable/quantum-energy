# v3.77 decision — quantum geometry branch under method dependence

## Decision

Keep the quantum-geometry branch alive, but **do not select polypentacene as a platform material yet**.

The reason is not lack of theoretical upside. It is that the discrete material topology assignment is method dependent in the published evidence: PBE/DFT and a cited GW-level treatment place the N=5 transition differently.

## Capital gate

Before any material purchase, custom strain fixture, patterned dielectric stack or non-uniform-field device is justified for this branch, require:

1. one hash-pinned relaxed N=5 structure;
2. converged PBE and GW-level parity/topology calculations on that identical structure;
3. fitted SSH parameters with uncertainty/model residuals;
4. lowest-exciton `P_exc` and quantum metric computed prospectively;
5. a predeclared rule for what constitutes method agreement.

If the discrete label remains unresolved, either:

- move to N=7 polyheptacene if both methods agree there and fabrication feasibility is acceptable; or
- retain the work as model/topology science and stop platform spend.

## New platform concept: digital topology + analog geometry

If a robust material is found, separate the controls:

- **strain / bond modulation** = candidate digital topology switch;
- **dielectric screening** = analog geometry/spread tuner;
- **sink spacing / quenching interface** = conversion/load variable;
- **temperature/disorder** = environment robustness axes.

This yields a prospective control cube rather than a single optimized film:

`topology state x quantum-geometry setting x sink load`.

A successful platform would show a prospectively predicted sink-response surface whose topology-dependent component survives controls for absorption, lifetime, ordinary dispersion, morphology and defects.

## Economic implication

The attractive end product is not “a topological material.” It is a **programmable exciton-routing layer** in which a small external or processing control changes how far neutral excitation can travel before irreversible conversion. That could matter for photovoltaic collection, luminescent concentrators, sensing, photochemistry or excitonic interconnects.

But the value proposition exists only if the control is reproducible under manufacturable disorder and if absolute useful work improves. Topology/geometry observables alone are not a product metric.

## Current spend recommendation

- Computational reproduction: YES.
- Author/source-data request: YES.
- Commodity compute / open-source electronic-structure workflow: YES when inputs are frozen.
- New strain hardware: NO.
- New field-gradient microfabrication: NO.
- Material campaign claiming topological polypentacene: NO.

## Promotion condition

Promote from QG0c to QG0d only after the discrete material topology is robust enough that phonon-limited diffusion is a meaningful downstream quantity rather than a method-choice artifact.
