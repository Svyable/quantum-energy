# v3.74 venture/research decision — open a quantum-geometry branch, but keep spend gated

## Decision

Add **QG-ENAQT: quantum geometry × environment co-design** as an exploratory platform branch.

Do **not** divert fabrication capital from the current OPV useful-work program yet. The branch earns capital only by clearing a material-level reproduction gate.

## Why it could create disproportionate value

If quantum geometry produces a real diffusion enhancement that survives room-temperature phonons/disorder, it attacks a fundamental loss channel—finite-lifetime excitons failing to reach a sink—without requiring long-lived quantum memory.

The first-passage model shows why this is potentially nonlinear in value: when sink distance is several reference diffusion lengths, even a moderate diffusion increase can multiply capture probability.

This would expand the platform from

`Hamiltonian + bath + cavity/field + sink`

to

`Hamiltonian + quantum geometry + bath + cavity/field + sink`.

That is a more ambitious and differentiated design space.

## Capital gates

### Gate 0 — free

- reproduce v3.74;
- independently reproduce or benchmark one published topological-exciton transport result;
- identify a credible trivial/control material or tunable phase comparison.

### Gate 1 — low-cost computational/material screen

Proceed only if the independent calculation supports a meaningful transport headroom and the control does not trivially differ in lifetime/defect quality.

### Gate 2 — transport experiment

Measure diffusion and lifetime separately. No sink or device claim yet.

### Gate 3 — geometric discriminator

Use non-uniform field response or another justified direct quantum-geometry observable while controlling dissociation/free-carrier artifacts.

### Gate 4 — useful sink

Only after Gates 1–3, add a quencher/electrical sink and test prospective capture gain.

### Gate 5 — active routing

Only after sink benefit exists, evaluate a reconfigurable optical/electric potential landscape for routing energy among multiple sinks.

## Kill conditions

Stop or narrow if:

- independent material calculations do not reproduce a useful transport advantage;
- matched controls show lifetime/defect/morphology explains the result;
- realistic environment penalties erase `G_eff>1`;
- bulk transport enhancement disappears at interfaces;
- no prospective sink-capture improvement follows from measured transport;
- synthesis/EHS/stability/manufacturing burden dominates the potential gain.

## Business implication

If validated, the differentiated asset is not a single topological material. It is an **open design rule for geometry-assisted, environment-robust energy routing** plus reproducible screening/control methods. That fits an open-source ecosystem better than secrecy-dependent material discovery.
