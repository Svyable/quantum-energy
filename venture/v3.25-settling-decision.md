# v3.25 deployment decision — qualify settling before randomized-order inference

## Decision

Do not spend interpretive effort on randomized-order R2 curvature until intensity-step settling has been qualified under the exact measurement configuration.

The sequence is now:

1. qualify the facility path and calibration/spectral/temperature controls;
2. measure repeated large-step `Voc(t)` transients;
3. derive a demonstrated dwell with v3.25;
4. use at least that dwell in the randomized-order discriminator;
5. only then compare monotonic and drift-adjusted curvature.

## Why this improves capital efficiency

Randomized order is scientifically valuable only if a change in intensity behaves like a controlled change in the independent variable. If each jump carries unresolved source/DUT/instrument memory, more randomized measurements can create a larger, slower dataset without increasing causal clarity.

A short settling qualification can therefore prevent wasted facility time and false mechanism interpretation.

## Synthetic planning result

For the frozen curvature metric, the existing 0.01 curvature-bias budget corresponds to a conservative per-point residual transient limit of approximately **69.5 microvolt**.

Under a purely synthetic one-time-constant model and a conservative 0.0954 V full-span step, required dwell ranges from about 3.6 s at `tau=0.5 s` to 72.2 s at `tau=10 s`.

These are not vendor/device performance values. The actual dwell must be measured.

## Operational gate

A facility randomized-order run is green only if:
- all required step directions pass v3.25;
- the chosen dwell is no shorter than the worst qualified step class;
- source, detector, gain/range, DUT temperature, contact, spectrum, and software configuration match qualification;
- the dwell is operationally compatible with degradation and total acquisition time.

If the dwell needed to bound settling is impractically long, do not relax the gate merely to finish the experiment. Reframe the work as dynamic/history-dependent transport/metrology and model that system explicitly.

## Commercial boundary

This is metrology infrastructure, not product differentiation and not quantum-mechanism evidence. Its commercial value is indirect: reliable causal measurements reduce false positives before proprietary modern-OPV materials/process spending.

## Safety / EHS

Large light-intensity steps and long dwell times can increase optical/thermal exposure. Facility interlocks, DUT temperature limits, source duty limits, and degradation criteria remain controlling. A randomized schedule must never override them.
