# v3.22 facility handoff decision

## Decision

Do not allow a real R2 facility dataset into confirmatory analysis unless the handoff packet passes the v3.22 integrity/completeness preflight.

## Business / deployment value

This is low-cost diligence infrastructure: it prevents later scientific, partner, and investor claims from depending on missing calibration evidence, detached source files, or silently mixed detector/source configurations. It also gives collaborators a concrete minimum export contract before bespoke data cleanup begins.

## Gate

- `PASS`: packet may proceed to downstream metrology qualification; no physical claim is implied.
- `INCOMPLETE`: request the missing evidence and stop confirmatory analysis.
- `FAIL`: resolve the contradiction/integrity problem and regenerate the packet from preserved source records.

## Claim boundary

The gate verifies data-room integrity and declared identity consistency only. It does not establish calibration correctness, uncertainty adequacy, device performance, EPC, or open-quantum transport.

## Next commercial-enabling increment

Use the contract with the first cooperating facility before data transfer so missing evidence is discovered before measurement work is interpreted or repeated.
