# v3.18 open deployment decision — covariance before mechanism

## Decision

Do not release an R2 mechanism claim from `Voc`-intensity curvature using an independence-only uncertainty budget.

The facility package must:
1. pass the v3.17 acquisition/qualification gates;
2. declare or estimate meaningful covariance components;
3. pass v3.18 uncertainty propagation;
4. retain adequate planning power after correlated uncertainty is included;
5. preserve independent substrate count separately from technical repeats.

## Why this matters

Common calibration uncertainty can be almost irrelevant to a derivative observable while a much smaller shape-dependent component can matter. Treating every calibration row as independent can therefore be wrong in either direction: it can exaggerate a common systematic or understate a correlated shape mode.

This is both a scientific-integrity and capital-efficiency improvement. It tells collaborators which calibration work actually reduces uncertainty in the decision-driving observable.

## Open-source value

The signed latent-component sidecar is intentionally facility-neutral and auditable. A collaborating lab can publish:
- the raw measurement export;
- the component definitions/loadings;
- the resulting machine-readable uncertainty output;
- the exact code version.

Downstream groups can then replace our assumptions with their own empirically supported covariance components without changing the physics-analysis code.

## Cost implication

No new instrument purchase is justified by this increment. The near-term value is better use of existing calibration/reference measurements and a clearer specification for what a facility must report.

## Claim boundary

This is metrology infrastructure. It does not establish quantum transport, EPC control, commercial energy gain, or a unique recombination mechanism.
