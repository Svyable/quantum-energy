# v3.28 Facility Partner Decision

## Decision

Do **not** select or publicly describe any audited organization as a complete R2 execution partner based only on public webpages.

The current source-linked matrix confirms only reference-detector traceability and spectral characterization across the three-candidate set. Five of seven v3.27 capability classes remain unresolved everywhere reviewed: detector/source linearity characterization, the prospective repeatability campaign, electrical-step temporal qualification, optical/DUT step qualification, and the exact frozen `Voc`-intensity acquisition protocol.

## Commercial / program implication

The next bottleneck is not another synthetic metrology model. It is a standardized evidence-return process for partner qualification. A false-positive partner choice risks invalidating prospective holdout logic, splitting calibration/configuration provenance across incompatible systems, or discovering late that required raw exports and timing controls are unavailable.

## Partner-selection gate

A candidate may advance to scheduling only after a frozen questionnaire returns dated evidence for every decision-critical field as one of:

- `CONFIRMED` with explicit configuration/evidence details;
- `CONDITIONAL` with stated limits and a documented bridge plan;
- `UNAVAILABLE`.

Unanswered fields remain `INCOMPLETE` and cannot be inferred from equipment lists or institutional reputation.

## Fairness / anti-marketing rule

The same capability questions and evidence standard must be used for every candidate in the comparison. Requirements may not be relaxed after seeing a favored facility's response. This is a technical procurement/partnership qualification, not a prestige ranking.

## Cost and schedule boundary

No vendor/facility quote, lead time, or scheduling commitment was obtained in v3.28. Therefore no cost or calendar ranking is reported. Those become decision variables only when sourced directly and versioned.

## Safety / provenance boundary

A multi-facility workflow is acceptable only if configuration-dependent measurements can be bridged without invalidating the frozen R2 gate dependencies. Facility safety rules and sample/material shipping constraints remain controlling.

## Kill / narrow gate

If no candidate can execute all configuration-coupled temporal and `Voc` measurements on the same frozen acquisition path, narrow the plan to a split workflow only after an explicit bridge study quantifies transfer uncertainty. If that bridge uncertainty consumes the R2 curvature/voltage-loss budget, stop the split-site plan rather than hiding the transfer term.

## Single best next increment

Create a machine-generated, identical facility confirmation questionnaire from v3.27 and a parser that converts returned responses into the same capability matrix without manual reinterpretation.
