# Venture decision — R2 cross-facility transfer v3.34

## Decision

Do not use a second facility's R2 measurements in platform, investor, or commercialization claims until the facility pair passes a prospective same-device A→B→A transfer screen under frozen configurations.

## Why this matters commercially

A transfer standard has more deployment value if its result is portable across qualified sites. A one-way A→B agreement is weak diligence evidence because device ageing, shipping, handling, and elapsed time are confounded with facility bias. The return-to-A leg creates a more defensible operational screen at low incremental complexity.

## Gate

For at least three complete qualified R2 primary devices, compute the drift-interpolated facility residual from v3.34. Screening PASS requires both absolute mean residual and RMS residual <=5 mV. The threshold is an engineering assumption aligned to the current R2 transfer/repeatability scale; it is not a standards certification or statistical-equivalence claim.

## Negative-result value

A FAIL is commercially useful. It identifies that cross-site data cannot yet be pooled and protects the program from selling a portability claim that could be explained by calibration, remounting, shipping, or device-history effects. Failed devices and deviations remain in the public record.

## Cost / scale implication

No new facility price or schedule is claimed. The v3.34 return leg adds real logistics and instrument time that must be incorporated into facility-specific timing/quote work rather than assumed free. Open PR #24 remains separate non-canonical timing work until reviewed.

## Next deployment gate

After both facilities individually satisfy prerequisite metrology gates, execute v3.34 prospectively. Only a successful reviewed transfer should support cross-facility pooling or a stronger R2 transferability claim; failure should trigger calibration/history diagnosis before expansion to additional sites.
