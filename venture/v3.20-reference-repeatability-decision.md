# v3.20 deployment decision — prospective calibration before DUT mechanism spend

## Decision

Do not treat an empirical calibration covariance model as experimentally supported until it predicts an untouched reference-detector holdout campaign.

The next metrology spend should therefore purchase or reserve **repeatability campaign time**, not additional mechanism-complexity hardware.

## Campaign gate

The preregistered campaign is:
- 24 training sessions;
- 6 prospective holdout sessions acquired only after model/QC/scoring freeze;
- 4 sweeps/session;
- 2 ascending + 2 descending sweeps/session;
- 30 sessions / 120 sweeps / 2,040 grid-point records before dark/anchor rows.

The count is based on synthetic variance-component precision planning, not a vendor quote or a claim about actual facility throughput.

## Why this is high value

The two open v3.19 PRs show that software can estimate calibration covariance in more than one plausible way. The key venture risk is no longer whether another estimator can be written; it is whether a frozen estimator transfers to new sessions under operational conditions.

A failed prospective holdout is economically useful. It prevents proprietary-material fabrication, extra R2 substrates, or instrument capex from being justified by an unstable calibration model.

A successful holdout does not prove device mechanism, but it removes one measurement-system uncertainty from the critical path and makes later FTPS/EL/Voc-intensity work more investable and reproducible.

## Capital boundary

No new equipment purchase is authorized by v3.20. Facility time, staff time, and calibration artifacts should be requested with explicit scope before hardware acquisition. No cost figure is asserted because no vendor/facility quote was obtained in this increment.

## Open-source value

The campaign template, randomization generator, precision calculation, raw schema, QC rules, and negative-result policy are public and facility-neutral. A partner can execute the same structure and publish raw repeats even if its detector/source hardware differs.

## Kill / narrow rule

If held-out sessions fail the frozen score, narrow the covariance claim and redesign the measurement model. Do not consume the failed holdout as tuning data and then present the same sessions as prospective validation.
