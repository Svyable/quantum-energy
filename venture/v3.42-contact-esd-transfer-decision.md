# Venture decision gate — R2 contact-state / ESD transfer control v3.42

## Decision

Do not interpret or commercially package a transferred R2 result as clean cross-session/cross-facility evidence when contact-state/ESD control is `FAIL` or `INCOMPLETE`.

## Why this matters commercially

A facility comparison that is actually driven by changed terminal contact, remount geometry, contamination, ESD/EOS, or package damage can create false confidence, unnecessary capital spend, or an incorrect vendor/facility ranking. A low-cost matched PRE/POST electrical-state fingerprint is therefore a diligence gate before scaling external campaigns.

## Spend gate

Before paying for a larger multi-facility R2 campaign, require:

1. a prospectively frozen safe dark-I–V configuration with device/instrument provenance;
2. characterized fixture/contact procedure;
3. characterized ESD sentinel with documented placement and response interpretation;
4. repeatability data sufficient to propose configuration-specific PRE/POST limits;
5. raw-data and analysis-commit retention;
6. `PASS` on the qualified dummy/reference configuration before primary mechanism-facing transfer.

Unknown thresholds, unknown sentinel response, or missing covariance provenance remain `INCOMPLETE`. They are not zero-risk assumptions.

## Claim boundary

A PASS reduces one operational confound. It does not demonstrate ESD immunity, shipping reliability, facility equivalence, improved photovoltaic performance, or any quantum mechanism.

## Kill / narrow rule

If a transferred primary shows an out-of-limit electrical-state shift or sentinel/QC failure, pause pooling and mechanism-facing interpretation. Diagnose contact, fixture, contamination, ESD/EOS, ageing, temperature, and instrument alternatives first. Preserve the failed transfer as useful negative operational evidence.

## Best next commercial increment

Collect dummy-level PRE/POST repeatability and characterized-sentinel evidence under the actual transfer fixture, then freeze evidence-backed acceptance limits before expanding paid facility work.
