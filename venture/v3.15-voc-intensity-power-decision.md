# v3.15 deployment decision — measure curvature only when measurement power is real

The R2 illumination measurement is now a staged engineering gate rather than an
automatic characterization checkbox.

## Capital / time rule

Do not increase device count to compensate for a noisy `Voc` measurement.
First demonstrate <=0.5 mV point-estimate uncertainty and acceptable
illumination/temperature stability.

The 17-point design is intentionally modest: it preserves local curvature
information while avoiding unnecessary optical dose. Extra substrates are
released only if measured between-substrate curvature variance makes the
five-substrate pilot underpowered.

## Open-source value

The power script publishes the grid-selection and sample-size assumptions in a
form another laboratory can modify for its own noise and device variance.
Null results remain useful: a flat, adequately powered curve constrains
recombination explanations rather than being discarded.
