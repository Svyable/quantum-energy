# v3.73 decision — baseline capability before treatment spend

## Decision

Do not unblind or scale A2/B2 stress-tomography work until A0/B0 baseline capability has been measured and the future interaction is shown to be resolvable at a feasible independent-lot count.

## Why this creates value

A four-arm durability/mechanism experiment is expensive in materials, fabrication slots, stress-chamber time, metrology and interpretation. If baseline lot-to-lot dispersion is already too large, adding treatment arms produces ambiguous data rather than information.

v3.73 therefore moves a key decision **earlier**:

1. measure A0/B0 baseline repeatability under both T and L;
2. compute the interaction-detection capability frontier;
3. choose a physical effect margin from useful-work relevance and real measurement capability;
4. select N before A2/B2 unblinding;
5. stop or improve the process if the useful margin is not resolvable.

## What is not optimized

The program does not choose the smallest statistically detectable effect as the business/scientific target. A tiny detectable ratio-of-ratios change may have no useful electrical value.

The physical margin must remain tied to stabilized Pmax, field-generation robustness, confound controls and durability relevance.

## Cost implication

This increment justifies **no new instrument purchase**. It uses the existing planned A0/B0 controls and stress pathways to decide whether the larger four-arm experiment is information-efficient.

## Kill rule

If measured baseline variability, stress-history mismatch, donor-free film integrity, contact drift or optical drift prevents a useful interaction from being resolved with a feasible lot count, do not proceed to a strong dual-path resilience claim. Improve the process or narrow the experiment first.
