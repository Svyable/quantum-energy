# Quantum Energy Venture Lab

Evidence-first research and venture-development repository for programmable open-quantum transport, organic photovoltaics, exciton/polariton transport, electron–phonon coupling, manufacturing controls, metrology, and commercialization.

## Working rules

- Separate **established evidence**, **engineering assumptions**, **hypotheses**, and **novel invention concepts**.
- Prefer falsifiable experiments and explicit kill/narrow gates over narrative-only progress.
- Do not treat long-lived room-temperature electronic coherence as a prerequisite for photosynthetic-inspired transport.
- Do not claim a universal quantum computer or commercially competitive PV performance without evidence.
- Keep commodity metrology and published device architectures distinct from prospective proprietary process-control/IP layers.
- Every automated research session should land on a branch and open a human-review pull request; never merge automatically.

The detailed research history, specifications, and automation instructions are maintained under `research/`, `technical/`, `venture/`, and `automation/`.
