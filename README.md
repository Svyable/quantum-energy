# Quantum Energy Venture Lab

Open, evidence-first research and venture-development repository for programmable open-quantum transport, organic photovoltaics, exciton/polariton transport, electron–phonon coupling, manufacturing controls, metrology, and commercialization.

## Mission

This technology is being developed in public for broad societal benefit. The project should optimize for **truth, reproducibility, useful negative results, and transferable engineering knowledge**, not for protecting a narrative. Strong claims are earned through transparent calculations, adversarial controls, uncertainty analysis, and prospective validation.

## Working rules

- Separate **established evidence**, **engineering assumptions**, **falsifiable hypotheses**, **synthetic/model results**, **experimental results**, and **novel invention concepts**.
- Prefer falsifiable experiments and explicit kill/narrow gates over narrative-only progress.
- Independently recompute decision-driving calculations and check units, limiting cases, uncertainty, sensitivity, and numerical convergence before publication.
- Do not treat long-lived room-temperature electronic coherence as a prerequisite for photosynthetic-inspired transport.
- Do not claim a universal quantum computer or commercially competitive PV performance without evidence.
- Keep conventional optical, morphological, electrostatic, thermal, contact, tunneling, measurement, and statistical explanations alive until discriminating evidence rules them down.
- Keep commodity metrology and published device architectures distinct from prospective process-control/invention layers.
- Keep negative/null results and visible correction history in the public record.
- Every automated research session should land on a branch and open a human-review pull request; never merge automatically.

## License status

The repository is intentionally public/open, but public visibility alone is not a complete open-source license. Before the first formal release, the project should explicitly choose and commit appropriate licenses for software, data/documentation, and hardware/CAD so reuse terms and contributor expectations are unambiguous.
