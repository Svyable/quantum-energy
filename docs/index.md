# Quantum Energy Venture Lab

Open, evidence-first research and engineering on programmable open-quantum transport and adjacent energy/materials systems, including environment-assisted quantum transport (ENAQT), exciton/polariton transport, organic photovoltaics, electron-phonon coupling, spectroscopy, metrology, manufacturing controls, and reproducible scientific software.

## Scientific boundary

This is active research. The project does **not** currently claim:

- a validated commercial “quantum energy” breakthrough;
- a universal room-temperature quantum computer;
- a unique quantum explanation for every photovoltaic or transport effect;
- commercially superior power conversion or lifetime.

Models and synthetic benchmarks are not measurements. `main` is the canonical reviewed record; open pull requests are review candidates.

## Agent-discovery benchmark

The v3.41 benchmark prospectively measures whether an unfamiliar agent starting from only the public project URL can find canonical state **and preserve claim boundaries**. Critical overclaims force a FAIL even if navigation is otherwise complete.

The benchmark is a software/discovery protocol. A high score does not validate the underlying science.
