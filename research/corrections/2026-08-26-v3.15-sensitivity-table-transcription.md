# Correction — v3.15 sensitivity-table transcription

## What happened

During the v3.15 session, the first committed copy of
`models/r2_voc_intensity_power_v3_15.csv` was populated manually from planning
calculations. Several substrate-sensitivity rows for `n=7`, `n=9`, and `n=12`
did not exactly match the output of the committed executable generator.

The mismatch was discovered during the same session, before advancing the human
review PR, by comparing selected committed rows against a fresh execution of
`models/r2_voc_intensity_power_v3_15.py`.

## Correction

The CSV was replaced with the exact generator output. The script was also
changed to write LF line endings explicitly (`lineterminator="\n"`) so CI can
perform an exact cross-platform text diff without repeating the project's prior
newline-normalization failure mode.

## Scientific impact

The principal v3.15 conclusions were not changed:

- 17-point / 7-point nominal power at 0.5 mV point noise and a 0.10 curvature
  effect remains `0.993795964` analytically;
- the 30,000-repetition Monte Carlo remains `0.992733333`;
- null false-positive rate remains `0.049833333`;
- five-substrate power remains about `0.815` for between-substrate SD 0.075 and
  about `0.588` for SD 0.100 at a 0.10 mean effect;
- the metrology gate and qualitative sample-size decision are unchanged.

Some non-primary sensitivity rows were incorrect in the superseded commit and
must not be cited. The corrected CSV plus executable generator are canonical.

## Prevention

The new CI workflow regenerates the CSV from code on Python 3.12, 3.13, and
3.14 and requires an exact diff to pass. Future tabular model outputs should be
committed from executable generation rather than hand transcription.
