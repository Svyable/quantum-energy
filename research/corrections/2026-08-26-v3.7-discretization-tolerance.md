# Correction — v3.7 photon-conservation discretization tolerance

## Error

During development of `r2_raw_spectrum_harness.py`, the first self-test required relative photon-integral agreement `<1e-10` after transforming a spectrum between wavelength and energy coordinates on a 1 nm grid. The implementation returned approximately `8.647e-7` and correctly failed.

## Diagnosis

The transform itself was correct. The mismatch came from numerical quadrature on different nonuniform coordinate grids.

Grid refinement produced:

- 2 nm: `3.458946e-6`
- 1 nm: `8.647364e-7`
- 0.5 nm: `2.161841e-7`
- 0.25 nm: `5.404602e-8`

The error falls by approximately 4x each time the wavelength step is halved, consistent with second-order trapezoidal convergence.

## Correction

The publication self-test is now relative mismatch `<2e-6` at 1 nm plus a convergence ratio between 3.5 and 4.5 for 2→1 nm and 1→0.5 nm.

## Impact

No prior experimental or physical result is affected. The incorrect threshold was caught before the harness was committed. The correction removes false precision and makes the numerical gate traceable to convergence behavior.
