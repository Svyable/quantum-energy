# Correction — v3.45 frozen CSV last-digit serialization

Date: 2026-08-27

## What failed

The first GitHub Actions run of `marcus-stark-field-robustness` (run `33138666948`) failed the exact generated-vs-frozen CSV comparison before the independent arithmetic step.

## Cause

Three `abs_log_orientation_asymmetry` cells in the manually frozen CSV differed from the production generator by one final displayed decimal digit:

- `lambda=0.25 eV, delta=0.01 eV`: `0.232090362431007` -> `0.232090362431006`
- `lambda=0.25 eV, delta=0.035 eV`: `0.812316268508523` -> `0.812316268508522`
- `lambda=0.25 eV, delta=0.07 eV`: `1.62463253701705` -> `1.62463253701704`

The mismatch arose because the initial CSV was frozen from a slightly different floating calculation/formatting path instead of being generated byte-for-byte by the committed serializer.

## Scientific impact

None of the decision-driving `R_worst` values, the 35 meV dimensional check, the analytic `lambda=-DeltaG` condition, sensitivity conclusions, claim boundaries, or prospective experimental gate changed.

This was a reproducibility-fixture serialization defect. The exact-CSV check worked as intended by refusing to label the fixture reproduced.

## Fix

The three final displayed digits were changed to match the deterministic committed generator. The CI tolerances and equations were **not** relaxed.

## Prevention

Future frozen numerical tables should be generated directly by the committed executable (`--write-expected`) and reviewed from the resulting diff rather than transcribed from an interactive calculation path.
