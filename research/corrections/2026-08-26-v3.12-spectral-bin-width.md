# Correction — v3.7 spectral-bin width was implicit

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Affected artifact:** `models/r2_raw_spectrum_harness.py` and its v3.7 interpretation for non-1-nm or real facility grids.

## Error

The v3.7 synthetic generator represented `N_lambda` as a wavelength spectral density but formed the detector-count signal from `N_lambda * throughput * integration_time` rather than `N_lambda * bin_width_nm * throughput * integration_time`. The inverse synthetic calibration likewise treated the recovered per-bin photon rate as a per-nanometre density without an explicit division by `bin_width_nm`.

## Why the default published numbers do not change

The frozen v3.7 fixture uses a **1 nm grid**. Multiplication/division by 1 nm leaves those default generated counts and recovered linewidths numerically unchanged. Therefore this correction does not alter the committed v3.7 1-nm Monte Carlo values or the v3.8 `DeltaVnr` benchmark.

The error matters for non-1-nm bins, nonuniform grids, and real facility exports.

## Independent negative control

The v3.12 synthetic facility fixture uses 2 nm bins. For M001:

- net detector rates = 100, 200, 300 count/s;
- radiometric factor = `1e8 emitted photons/count`;
- correct total emitted rate = `6e10 photons/s`.

Correct density handling divides each per-bin photon rate by `2 nm` and reintegrates by `2 nm`, returning `6e10 photons/s`.

The historical no-bin-width pattern instead integrates to `1.2e11 photons/s`, exactly **2x too large**. `tests/facility_export_gate.py` freezes this failure control.

## Correction

The historical v3.7 file remains frozen for provenance/regression of its 1-nm synthetic results. It is no longer the ingest specification for real facility spectra. Real/future facility data must carry explicit `bin_width_nm` and obey:

- `photon_rate_per_bin = net_count_rate * emitted_photons_per_count`;
- `photon_density_per_nm = photon_rate_per_bin / bin_width_nm`;
- total rate = `sum(photon_density_per_nm * bin_width_nm)`.

## Downstream impact

**Unchanged:** frozen v3.7 default 1-nm recovery, v3.8 published equation benchmark, and v3.9-v3.11 provenance infrastructure.

**Superseded:** any interpretation that v3.7's synthetic count equation was dimensionally general for arbitrary spectral bin widths.

**Still blocked:** empirical facility sensitivity/linewidth uncertainty and H1-H4 physical mechanism decisions.

This is a software/units correction, not experimental evidence about R2, EPC, static disorder, or energy-conversion performance.
