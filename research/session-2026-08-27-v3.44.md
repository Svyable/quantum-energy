## Quantitative verification

- `T = 300 +/- 1 K`
- `EQE_EL = 1e-6`, 10% relative 1-sigma uncertainty

Governing equation:

`DeltaV_nr = -(k_B T/q) ln(EQE_EL)`.

Analytic first-order derivatives:

- `|dV/dEQE| u(EQE) = (k_B T/q) u(EQE)/EQE`
- `|dV/dT| u(T) = |-(k_B/q) ln(EQE)| u(T)`

- `DeltaV_nr = 357.1585759879732 mV`
- EQE-relative contribution `= 2.5851999786 mV`

## Dimensional/limiting/model checks

- `(V/K)*K*ln(1) = V`.
- For `0<EQE<1`, loss is positive.
- `EQE=1` gives exactly zero loss.
- Nonpositive EQE and nonpositive temperature are rejected.
- First-order propagation is explicitly invalidated as a sufficient model when weak-signal uncertainty is strongly non-Gaussian or local linearization fails.

## Statistical independence

Uncertainty components are not experimental replicates. Session repeats, pixels, and integrations must remain nested under lot -> substrate -> device/pixel -> session -> measurement. Calibration-scale terms remain systematic unless independently demonstrated otherwise.

## Conventional/null explanation

A favorable apparent `DeltaV_nr` difference can be produced by calibration scale drift, background subtraction, temperature error, injection mismatch, or correlated session effects rather than a material/interface mechanism. The discriminator is a complete provenance-backed uncertainty/covariance budget plus orthogonal reciprocity and control measurements.
