## Narrow evidence conclusion

The reviewed public sources explicitly support only two of the seven v3.27 capability classes across the audited set: reference-detector traceability and spectral characterization. This does not mean the other five are unavailable. It means the exact R2 requirements are not established by the public evidence reviewed.

## Engineering assumptions

- Candidate webpages are sufficiently current to support a first contact matrix, but not a final execution decision.
- `KNOWN_AVAILABLE` is reserved for explicit public evidence close to the v3.27 capability class; exact configuration applicability still requires direct confirmation before acquisition.
- Missing public documentation is represented as `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`, never `UNAVAILABLE`.

## Conventional/null explanation

The dominant null explanation for an incomplete public matrix is simple documentation incompleteness: a lab can possess a capability without advertising it. The opposite failure is also possible: a listed instrument may not be available for the required custom protocol. Direct written confirmation against v3.27 is the discriminator.

## Negative result retained

No audited candidate is publicly evidenced as a complete single-site R2 execution partner, and combining the currently public-confirmed capabilities across all three still leaves five of seven capability classes unresolved. The program should not infer a complete partner from institutional reputation.
