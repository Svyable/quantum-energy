# Evidence note — dual-path stress tomography v3.72

## Established external evidence

### PY-IT can change BTP-eC9 morphology and thermal robustness

Song et al., *Matter* (2022), DOI `10.1016/j.matt.2022.03.012`, studied PM6:BTP-eC9:PY-IT films/devices. The paper reports that PY-IT forms entangled chain networks, suppresses BTP-eC9 diffusion/crystallization, and improves mechanical/thermal robustness. Devices were stored at 85 C in dark N2; the optimal ternary retained 85.5% initial PCE after 250 h and was reported ~7.2% higher than the binary system. Under 160 C / 4 h annealing, ternary morphology showed suppressed BTP-eC9 crystallization relative to binary.

Boundary: donor is PM6, not D18. These values are precedent and sensitivity anchors only.

### OPV stability protocols separate stress factors

Reese et al., *Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells* 95 (2011), DOI `10.1016/j.solmat.2011.01.036`, established consensus ISOS research procedures for shelf, light, thermal/weathering and cycling tests. The protocols are research comparison practices, not product qualification.

A 2026 OPV study, DOI `10.1039/D5TC03674G`, reports an ISOS-L-1 implementation on encapsulated cells under MPP tracking, 100 mW cm^-2 illumination and controlled 50 C. It also shows why light stress can expose ETL-specific degradation not visible from dark thermal storage alone.

## Project inference

Because PY-IT has direct BTP-eC9 thermal-morphology precedent, a B2 stability advantage could arise without any new charge-generation route. A two-stress A0/A2/B0/B2 design can therefore discriminate whether protection is:

- broad acceptor-domain/morphology stabilization;
- heterojunction-specific;
- mixed;
- or stress-specific.

This is a prospective inference design, not evidence that D18/PY-IT/eC9 has two generation routes.

## Model result

v3.72 defines log-retention difference-in-differences interactions and a stress-selectivity contrast. The synthetic fixture exists only to verify exact arithmetic and fail-closed behavior.

## Conventional explanations retained

- thermal morphology stabilization without route redundancy;
- contact/transport stabilization;
- optical/thickness drift;
- non-orthogonal stresses;
- donor-free morphology that does not represent B2;
- improved relative retention with worse absolute useful work.
