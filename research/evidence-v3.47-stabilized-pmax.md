## Negative / limiting findings encoded

- A voltage-loss improvement without robust stabilized-Pmax improvement is not useful-work validation.
- One negative lot fails the same-sign condition even when the average could remain favorable.
- Snapshot/scan Pmax cannot substitute for the prospective stabilized-MPP endpoint.

## Remaining conventional explanations

A real Pmax gain could still be caused by ordinary morphology/thickness optimization or contact/transport changes rather than EPC/interface physics. Conversely, transient tracking, irradiance/temperature mismatch, or area/contact errors could create a false apparent gain. Mechanism attribution therefore remains separate from this useful-work gate.
